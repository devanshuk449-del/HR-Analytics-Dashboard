# 📊 HR Analytics Dashboard

This repository contains an **HR Analytics Dashboard** project built using **Power BI** and **Excel**.  
It provides insights into employee attrition trends, workforce demographics, and salary distribution to help HR teams make data-driven decisions.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview
Employee attrition is a critical challenge for organizations. This dashboard analyzes HR data to identify:
- Attrition patterns across **age groups, gender, education, and job roles**
- Impact of **salary slabs, years at company, and work-life balance** on attrition
- Department-wise breakdown (Human Resources, Research & Development, Sales)

The goal is to provide actionable insights for **employee retention strategies**.

---

## 📂 Dataset
- **Source:** HR Analytics Excel Data (`HR Analytics Excel Data.xlsx`)
- **Records:** 1470 employees
- **Key Attributes:** Age, Gender, Department, Job Role, Salary, Education, Years at Company, Attrition status

Example fields:
- `Age`, `Gender`, `JobRole`, `MonthlyIncome`, `YearsAtCompany`, `Attrition`

---

## 📈 Dashboard Features
- **KPIs:**
  - Employee Count: **1470**
  - Attrition Count: **237**
  - Attrition Rate: **16.1%**
  - Avg Age: **36.9 years**
  - Avg Salary: **6.5K**
  - Avg Tenure: **7 years**

- **Visualizations:**
  - Attrition by **Gender, Age, Education, Salary Slab**
  - Attrition by **Years at Company**
  - Attrition by **Job Role** (e.g., Laboratory Technicians, Sales Executives, Research Scientists)
  - Department-wise attrition trends

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- **Power BI** → Dashboard creation & visualization
- **Excel** → Data preprocessing and cleaning
- **Data Analysis** → Attrition trend identification

---

## 📊 Key Insights
- Highest attrition among **Laboratory Technicians (62)** and **Sales Executives (57)**
- Majority attrition in **26–35 age group (116 employees)**
- Employees with **salary up to 5K** show the highest attrition (163 cases)
- Education fields **Life Sciences (38%)** and **Medical (27%)** dominate attrition share
